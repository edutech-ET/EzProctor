Exam Lifecycle

Simple End-To-End Workflow

  1. 1. Setup: Admin starts platform and checks system health.
  2. 2. Author: Teacher creates exam template and questions.
  3. 3. Schedule: Teacher creates session and assigns students.
  4. 4. Run: Students verify identity and launch secure IDE.
  5. 5. Monitor: Teacher tracks live progress and integrity signals.
  6. 6. Review: Submissions are inspected, graded, and exported.
